## Full Description
*(4,000 chars max — use this verbatim in Play Console)*

Ripple is a personal wellness tracker built for people who want to understand how every part of their life connects — sleep, glucose, mood, meals, movement, money, and more. Your data stays on your own server. No ads. No data selling. No subscriptions.

**Track everything that matters**

• Mood check-ins with optional notes — spot emotional patterns over time
• Live blood glucose from your Dexcom CGM — with spike alerts and trend arrows
• Steps, sleep, heart rate, exercise, weight, and oxygen saturation via Android Health Connect
• Meal logging with barcode scanning and AI food recognition (Passio)
• Water and substance intake (caffeine, alcohol)
• Spending categories and bank transactions (via Plaid) — see how money affects wellbeing
• Medications with daily reminder schedules
• Menstrual cycle tracking
• Books, hobbies, and custom lifestyle metrics

**AI insights that connect the dots**

Ripple's insight engine surfaces correlations you wouldn't find on your own — like how your sleep quality affects next-day glucose, or whether high-spend days follow poor sleep. Every insight is sourced from your own data only. Observations, never diagnoses.

Ask My Data: chat directly with your two-week data summary using a built-in AI assistant.

**Designed around your privacy**

Your data is stored on a server you control — not a shared cloud. Ripple never sells data, never shows ads, and includes zero analytics or ad tracking SDKs. Review our full Privacy Policy at https://app.kels.gg/privacy.

**Built for your daily rhythm**

• Home screen with daily AI summary, live glucose ring, step progress, and mood prompt
• Customisable tab bar — show only the trackers you use
• Fasting timer on the home screen
• Daily and weekly recap screens with score cards and trend charts
• Monthly recap with top insights for the month
• Medication reminders with per-medication schedules
• Quiet mode presets (meeting, cinema, focus) to pause notifications
• App Lock with biometric authentication
• Wear OS companion tiles: glance, log, and breathe tiles for your wrist

**Export and backup**

Export your full data as JSON or a PDF report. Back up automatically to Google Drive. Your data, your control.

**Integrations**

• Dexcom Share (CGM glucose)
• Android Health Connect (steps, sleep, HR, exercise, weight, SpO₂)
• Plaid (bank transactions)
• Google Drive (backup)
• Hardcover.app (book tracking)
• USDA FoodData Central + Open Food Facts (nutrition lookup)
• Passio Nutrition AI (food photo recognition)

**Health disclaimer:** Ripple is a personal wellness tracking tool, not a medical device. Insights are observations based on your own data — never medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.
